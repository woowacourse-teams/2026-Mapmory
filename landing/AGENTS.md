## Mapmory Landing Decisions

### Photo-finder hero (2026-10-07)

These decisions supersede the earlier hero guidance below (the editorial `여행의 순간을, 나만의 지도로.` hero, the desktop scroll relay, the mobile 1.8-second globe reveal, the first-viewport Figma direction, and "no separate how-it-works section"). The interactive globe section below remains unchanged (the Korea section was removed on 2026-10-08).

- Goal: visitors from campus community posts (mostly mobile) should think "신기한데 한번 써볼까?" and stop worrying about photo privacy. Usability tests found the wow point is the app finding the photos of one place inside a huge camera roll, not the 3D globe; testers worried the app reads or uploads every photo.
- The first viewport is one non-looping demo: a dense photo library scans, a place is typed, only that place's photos fly to the map and its region turns mint, then `21,474장 중 <장소> 사진 N장을 찾았어요`. Visitors replay it with the place chips. Reduced motion shows the finished state.
- Demo places only use units the shipping app can record: whole countries abroad and Korean 시·군·구 at home; the app cannot pick a 시·도 or Korea itself (`TripRecordDestination.kt`, same on main and develop). Known gap: the hero's 제주 chip fills the 시·도 KR-49 and should become 제주시 or 서귀포시 in a follow-up PR (currently 제주 / 일본 / 미국). Never show city or landmark search such as 오사카 until the app supports it.
- Keep the one-line trust promise beside the hero CTA and a separate trust + FAQ section before the final download section. Every trust sentence must match the client code on both main and develop: finding photos runs on the device and compares each photo's stored location with the bundled boundary (develop also uses capture time to group and select, so never say location only or name the date as a criterion), only photos the user picks are uploaded when saving, and records can be deleted in the app. Do not promise that deleting a record removes uploaded files from storage until the backend does so.
- Section order: hero demo -> how-it-works scroll story (`#how`) -> interactive globe -> trust heading + FAQ -> download.
- 2026-10-08: the Korea add-to-map section (17-province map, district level 3, example photos, `대한민국 지도` nav link) was removed because the how-it-works demo now carries the pick-photos-and-fill loop. The Korea-experience bullets further down are historical; do not rebuild that section without a new decision. (2026-10-12: the #how story now ends on the three-level map fill instead.)
- 2026-10-07 design pass: every hero library tile is a real photo (never gradient stand-ins), the payoff line is LINE Seed Bold without Nanum Pen or glow, and the result lands as a pill over the grid so the store buttons and trust line stay in the first mobile viewport. Headings are one colour (no mint-accented phrase). Solid mint is kept for found/filled map state and primary actions (download buttons and the in-map CTAs); small mint text, step numbers and labels use `--accent-ink`. Below the hero every section h2 uses `--type-h2`. The on-device -> on-save boundary now lives in the #how story (beat 02 and beat 04 sub lines); the old `.trust-flow` panel is gone and the trust section is its heading plus the FAQ (2026-10-08: record deletion moved into the FAQ as "남긴 기록은 지울 수 있나요?").
- 2026-10-08: hero and how-it-works photos are openly licensed (CC0, public domain or CC BY only; no BY-SA/NC) EXIF-stripped webp files in `public/assets/photos/`, each listed in `src/data/photo-credits.json` and shown under the footer's `사진 출처`. The story's found photos are `gyeongbuk-01..04`; `gangwon-02` (a Buddha statue), `gangwon-04` (a photo-frame installation) and `busan-04` (painted mural walls) were deleted with their credits on 2026-10-12 because an artwork is their subject. No sculptures, artworks, logos or watermarks as the subject: a CC license on the photo does not clear the artwork's own copyright.
- Superseded 2026-10-12 by the app tour below. How-it-works was a three-tap phone demo (measured as `how_play`), not paragraphs: pick a Korean province (부산 / 강원 / 경주, none of the hero places) -> tap the photos to keep (nothing starts selected, like the app; 모두 선택 picks all) -> save fills the province with a stamp (the stamp names the place, the result line names the painted province, e.g. 경주 -> "경북이 칠해졌어요"). - Mechanics: a tall track (`calc(530svh - 80px)` on phones; `clamp(3200px, 430svh, 3800px)` plus the sticky frame's height from 561px wide) holds one sticky frame. Every frame is a pure function of scroll progress, so scrolling back up replays it exactly; typing, tap rings, checks and number swaps are threshold-triggered `data-*` states with CSS transitions, so they revert on scroll-back. One passive scroll listener (attached only while the track is near, via IntersectionObserver), one rAF per event, transform/opacity/text/`data-*` writes only (2D transforms), `will-change` only under `.is-live` and only in the stage states where that node moves (see Performance). The card chrome in beat 04 is a threshold too (200ms fade). A jump across two or more taps (a fast flick, or a reload into the story) marks each tap it turns on `data-skip=1`, sticky until the reader scrolls back above it, so finished rings and pulses never replay; there is no stage-wide instant flag. No wheel/touch/key listeners, `preventDefault`, scroll-snap, `scrollTo`, `scrollIntoView` or timers that move the story. Each hold is at least 100px of scroll on phones and 120px from 561px wide. A running number is one text node in a fixed box (sized by an invisible copy of its final value and left-aligned, since LINE Seed has no tabular figures) that is a relayout boundary: size and layout containment on a box that is neither a flex nor a grid item, or every new value relays out the whole page. The in-phone counter and percent keep strict containment on full-width boxes with at least 20px to spare on each side. Nothing is written to a layer while it is invisible (the loading screen's counter and bar stop once the screen has gone; the overlay's only while it shows). On the camera push the ground leads (inward `e` .05-.35) and the screen's content and background go clear first (.10-.35) on one wrapper, before the bezel. `HOW_STORY_INWARD` is `zoom` (a shared-camera push into the screen).
- Performance: in Chromium any main-thread transform write re-layerizes the whole page on that frame (opacity writes on a `will-change` layer do not), so the per-frame cost follows the page's layer count; keep promoted layers few. Traps found in traces and kept out: a rounded `overflow` clip inside a sliding screen repaints that screen every frame; SVG `<text>` under a scaling ancestor relays out every frame (the map tab's content, its `.how-map-gate`, skips layout with `content-visibility: hidden` until beat 05; on the gate, not the screen, because a skipped subtree keeps its old style and the gate's animation would not restart); a running animation promotes the later siblings that overlap it (the spinner paints last via `z-index`); an opacity scrub on a large non-promoted layer re-rasters it, and an opacity on the library sprite split its panels into several large layers (the library dims under a ground-coloured cover, `.how-mosaic-dim`, and the pins' inner box clips its panels to the window). Mid-swap, while a copy or state transition runs, counts briefly reach about 17-22 (the same before and after that change). Re-measure after any stage change. Ship gate: a real mid-range Android must play the push and the count without visible drops; CPU-throttled headless runs (4x/6x) are only a proxy, and switching `HOW_STORY_INWARD` to `fade` does not remove the per-frame layerization (the screens and the pin wave still move; on 2026-10-12 it measured no better than `zoom` at 4x).
