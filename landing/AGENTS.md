### How-it-works scroll story (2026-10-12)

The owner (티온) decided on 2026-10-12 to replace the 3-tap toy (1 of 6 viewers started it) with a scroll-driven story. For `#how` only, this overrides "Do not add a separate how-it-works section" and the 340svh scroll guidance written for the old hero; the story is not a restatement because it shows what no other section shows: how the phone finds one trip's photos inside a huge library and what leaves the phone, and when.

- The point is the Wow (speed: however big the library, the phone gets through all of it; ease: pick a place and save), with one short reassurance line per scene. h2 `HOW_STORY_TITLE` (사진첩이 커도, 장소만 고르면 돼요.); the owner may swap in the team's Wow sentence.
- Five scenes, one headline (at most 20 characters) and one support line (at most 40) each: 장소만 골라요 (type 경주, pick 경상북도 경주시) -> 폰이 사진첩을 전부 훑어요 (the library scrolls past a scan line, pin chips or 위치 없음, the 확인한 사진 counter runs to 21,474) -> 그곳에서 찍은 사진만 남아요 (dots on the bundled 경주시 boundary, 4 turn mint, pill `21,474장 중 경주시 사진 4장`) -> 골라서 저장하면 고른 사진만 올라가요 (sheet with the 4 photos, 3 checked, 저장 tapped, 3 copies rise across the line, one stays tagged 폰에 그대로) -> 지도가 칠해져요 (경주시·경상북도·대한민국).
- Scene 1 has no 사진 찾기 tap: picking the place is what starts the search (develop starts it on the pick; main needs 이 장소 사진 찾기, which the story leaves out rather than shows wrongly). The place search runs on the server on develop (Places proxy), so the 내 폰 안 frame and chip stay hidden during scene 1 and fade in only as the card leaves and the scan starts.
- The 저장 in scene 4 stands for saving the record, not for a button right after the pick (main also asks for 선택한 사진 추가, a title and a start date; develop's picker saves with 기록하기). The screen-reader text says the 3 photos go up when the record is saved (그 뒤 기록을 저장하면), never that saving follows the pick at once.
- Dots and flags: every dot clears the drawn 경주시 boundary by at least 9 logical px (inside or outside), and the 4 경주시 dots sit at least 18 px apart. Pin chips appear as each tile crosses the scan line (one clipped pin layer, not per-flag writes). Fillers reuse only the `elsewhere` photos (pinned) and busan-02 (always 위치 없음); a found 경주 photo never repeats as a filler, and no two side-adjacent cells share a photo.
- Stage, top to bottom: a server strip holding only `Mapmory 서버 받은 사진 n장` (0 until the copies arrive), the dashed line labelled `사진은 저장할 때만 이 선을 넘어요`, and the dashed 내 폰 안 zone with the 경상북도 경주시 chip, the hero's 예시 badge and, under them, the `확인한 사진 n / 21,474장` counter (plus the measured timer and tap counter when set). Everything above the line reads as the server side, so a phone-side number (the scan count, a time, taps) never sits there: on both app versions the check runs on the phone, and a count of the whole library next to the server reads as "the server checked every photo". The 2026-10-12 honesty review moved the counter out of the strip, where amendments v2 had put it on the left; the owner has yet to confirm the new place. Never a device mockup.
- Photos: the 9 story files above (no hero file). Coordinates are checked against the app's own 47130 boundary (`src/data/gyeongju-boundary.json`, generated by `scripts/generate-korea-map.mjs gyeongju` from the client's `korea-districts-47.json`).
- Numbers: every measured number lives in `HOW_STORY_METRICS` and starts as `null`; null means its UI (timer, tap counter, footnote) is absent, never shown as 0. Only numbers the team measured on the shipping app go there, with their source. No literal time or tap claims in copy (`1분`, `초 만에`, `탭이면`, `번 탭` are banned by tests). 21,474 is the illustrative library under the 예시 badge. When a measured scan is set, the counter runs to the measured library and the pill drops the `N장 중` part (`경주시 사진 4장`), because the 4 found photos are the example's, not the measured library's.
- `taps` counts exactly the taps the story shows, from the search field to 저장: the field (optional), the place result, each photo tapped, then save. The + that opens a record is not shown and not counted. A count measured on main must stay null, because main also needs 이 장소 사진 찾기, 선택한 사진 추가, a title and a start date. A count outside what the story shows (5-6 taps for tap-to-check, 3-4 for prechecked) is reported in the plan's `errors` and the counter stays off.
- Selection: `HOW_STORY_SELECTION` is `tap-to-check` (develop: found photos start unchecked) or `prechecked` (main); both end with the same 3 photos checked. Switching is a one-constant change.
- Every claim must hold on both `origin/main` and `origin/develop` client code. Never show: a device mockup, a mint 저장 button, 모두 선택, a %, 올리는 중/업로드 중/저장 중, Nanum Pen in the sheet, date matching or autofill, location being stripped, a 10-photo cap, 원본 그대로, landing on the map after save, or storage deletion. The stage labels are illustrative, not the app's exact strings.
- Mechanics: a tall track (320svh on phones, `clamp(2300px, 340svh, 3000px)` from 561px wide, where a mouse wheel is likely) holds one sticky frame. Under 901px the stacked frame is sized to its content and centred in the sticky room (`--how-sticky-extra`), and the caption slot is capped (124px, 132px from 561px, 184px from 901px); a caption that overflows its slot switches the story to static. Every frame is a pure function of scroll progress, so scrolling back up replays it exactly. One passive scroll listener (attached only while the track is near, via IntersectionObserver), one rAF per event, transform/opacity/text writes only, `will-change` only under `.is-live`. No wheel/touch/key listeners, `preventDefault`, scroll-snap, `scrollTo`, `scrollIntoView` or timers that move the story. Captions swap by scene index with a short fade, never scrubbed. Each hold is at least 100px of scroll on a 360x640 phone and 120px at 1280x720.
- Static fallback (the same stage rendered at each scene's end, as a list): reduced motion, no IntersectionObserver, windows under 560px tall, a canvas scale below 0.8, or captions that overflow (large text). A static story re-checks on any width change, and on a height change only when the window grows more than 100px over its lowest height or crosses 560px (mobile toolbars do not flip it). The story is `aria-hidden`; each step carries a screen-reader description that adapts to the selection and metrics.
- Analytics: the story sends two passive reach events, an explicit exception to "keep illustrative motion out of interactive experience events": `how_story_view` (`story_mode=scroll|static`, once per page load after 1 s at 50% of the viewport, or right before the first `how_story_scene` if that comes first, as on a reload that restores the scroll into a hold) and `how_story_scene` (`scene_index=1-5`, scroll mode only, once per scene after 0.7 s in its hold). Never `experience_*` or `memory_open` for the story. The header 사용 방법 link sends `experience_cta_click` with `experience_type=how_story`.
